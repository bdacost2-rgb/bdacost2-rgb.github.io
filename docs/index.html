Built to let a human guide a robot into environments too dangerous to enter, this capstone pairs a 4-DOF arm with a wearable haptic glove. Integrated MediaPipe hand tracking, ROS 2 control, and Moteus CAN motor commands on a Jetson Orin Nano, with BLE carrying tactile feedback back to the operator.
